Privacy

This page covers what happens to your information on this website. It’s short because right now the site is a few pages about the company and our games — there’s no account to create and nothing to sign up for.

A full privacy policy covering our games will be published here before they launch, and in the app before you play.

What we collect here

When you visit this site we collect basic technical information automatically: your rough location (from your IP address), the type of device and browser you’re using, which pages you looked at, and where you arrived from. We use it to see whether the site works and which pages people find useful.

If you email or call us, we keep that message and your contact details so we can reply.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t ask for anything sensitive on this site, and there’s no reason to send us any.

Who else sees it

We use outside tools to host the site and to measure traffic and advertising, and those companies receive some of the information above as part of doing that. Our cookies page lists them.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email support@winjogames.com and we’ll help. You can also turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, and we’ll treat it as an opt-out.

Our games are for players 18 and over, and this site isn’t aimed at children.
