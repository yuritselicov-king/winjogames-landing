COMPANY

A mobile game company betting on skill.

Winjo Games develops and publishes competitive mobile games. In a category dominated by chance mechanics, we build matches where two players face the same board and the better player wins. Fairness is a design constraint for us, not a policy page.

Three things we refuse to compromise

  1. 01

    Fairness is engineering

    Deterministic boards, server-authoritative timing, matchmaking on demonstrated skill. Anti-cheat is owned by the team shipping features, not bolted on later.

  2. 02

    Short cycles

    We build in weekly loops: a change goes out, data comes back, the next change is built on what we learned. Nobody waits a quarter to find out if an idea worked.

  3. 03

    Balance decided by data

    Every tuning change is an experiment with a stated hypothesis. If a mechanic rewards spending over skill, it loses the argument.

How we are set up

TEAMS

A designer, an engineer, an artist and a data partner take a feature from idea to live. Fewer handoffs, clearer ownership.

DECISIONS

Arguments are settled with a test, not seniority. Every change states what it expects to move.

PLAYERS

Competitive integrity comes before short-term revenue. A player who feels cheated does not come back.
