Cookies

A cookie is a small file a website leaves on your browser so it can recognise you next time. This page explains the ones we use here. Our games are apps, not browser games, so none of this applies to playing — only to this website.

What we use them for

  • Keeping the site working. Basic cookies that let pages load and stay secure. These can’t be turned off.
  • Measuring traffic. Counting visits and seeing which pages people actually read, so we know what’s useful.
  • Advertising. Seeing whether our ads work, and showing Winjo Games ads to people who’ve been here before.

The full list of cookies, including who provides each one and how long it lasts, will be published here before launch.

Turning them off

Every browser lets you block or delete cookies — it’s in the settings, usually under privacy. Blocking everything will break parts of the site.

If you turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, we’ll treat it as you opting out of advertising cookies. You can also email support@winjogames.com and ask us directly.

In our games

Apps don’t use cookies. They use your device’s advertising ID instead, which you control in your phone’s settings — under Privacy & Security → Tracking on iPhone, or Google → Ads on Android.
